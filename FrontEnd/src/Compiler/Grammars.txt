Program --> Statement

Statement --> AssignmentStatement Statement
            | InputStatement Statement
            | OutputStatement Statement
            | IfStatement Statement
            | WhileStatement Statement
            | ε 
            

AssignmentStatement --> IDENTIFIER ASSIGN Expression

InputStatement --> INPUT IdentifierList

IdentifierList --> IDENTIFIER
                 | IDENTIFIER COMMA IdentifierList

OutputStatement --> OUTPUT OutputList

OutputList --> OutputItem
             | OutputItem COMMA OutputList

OutputItem --> Expression
             | LITERAL

IfStatement --> IF BooleanExpression Block ElseChain

ElseChain --> ELSE Block | ε
            | IFELSE BooleanExpression Block ElseChain

WhileStatement --> WHILE BooleanExpression Block

Block --> LEFT_BRACE Statement RIGHT_BRACE




Expression --> BooleanExpression
             | ArithmeticExpression

ArithmeticExpression --> Additive

Additive --> Multiplicative
           | Additive PLUS Multiplicative
           | Additive MINUS Multiplicative

Multiplicative --> Power
                 | Multiplicative MULTIPLY Power
                 | Multiplicative DIVIDE Power
                 | Multiplicative MODULO Power

Power --> Factor
        | Factor POWER Power

Factor --> IDENTIFIER
         | INTEGER
         | FLOAT
         | LEFT_PAREN ArithmeticExpression RIGHT_PAREN

BooleanExpression --> LogicalOr

LogicalOr --> LogicalAnd
            | LogicalOr OR LogicalAnd

LogicalAnd --> LogicalNot
             | LogicalAnd AND LogicalNot

LogicalNot --> Comparison
             | NOT LogicalNot

Comparison --> Constant
             | IDENTIFIER
             | ArithmeticExpression CompareOp ArithmeticExpression
             | LEFT_PAREN BooleanExpression RIGHT_PAREN  

Constant --> TRUE
           | FALSE

CompareOp --> EQUAL
            | NOT_EQUAL
            | LESS_THAN
            | GREATER_THAN
            | LESS_EQUAL
            | GREATER_EQUAL
